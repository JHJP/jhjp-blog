I am Jiheon (Jay) Park, a data scientist working on predictive modelling, causal inference,
and AI-orchestrated development workflows. I build production ML systems on Databricks with
PySpark and Spark SQL, and I use LLMs as adversarial reviewers and as code generators driven
by written specifications.

The work itself is on the [Portfolio](portfolio) page: five prediction and causal-inference
models delivered end to end at a leading global insurance company, a registered patent, and a
live asset-allocation service.

This blog documents my technical work: the mathematical architectures, engineering decisions, and AI-assisted workflows behind the models I build.
